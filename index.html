🧪 Grade 6 STEM Quick Science Guide

What is pH? Measures how acidic (0–6) or alkaline/basic (8–14) water is. Healthy fish and dam life thrive around 6.5 – 8.5 pH.
What is TDS & EC? Total Dissolved Solids (ppm) and Electrical Conductivity (µS/cm) count tiny dissolved minerals and salts in the water. Under 500 ppm is clean freshwater.
Why Turn Off Radios? Bluetooth and Wi-Fi stay off on the dam while the ESP32 sleeps for 3 hours at a time, letting one small battery and solar panel run for weeks!
